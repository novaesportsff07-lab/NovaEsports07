FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No entry fee and no payment, ever.

When do I get the Room ID?

On Telegram, 10 minutes before the match. Join within 5 minutes or your slot is given away.

No. Emulators, hacks and teaming mean disqualification.

Yes, with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Your slot is confirmed when we reply on Telegram. Send your registration message from the Join Free form.

What if the match is cancelled?
